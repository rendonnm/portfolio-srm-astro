I've always been curious about understanding how things work behind a screen. That curiosity led me to software development and, over time, to finding a point I love between **logic, product, and creativity**.

I'm a Computer Engineer from Medellín 🇨🇴, and I currently work as a **Frontend Software Engineer Specialist at Scotiabank**, developing digital banking solutions alongside international teams.

I mainly work with **React, Next.js, and TypeScript**, building modern, accessible, and scalable applications. I'm also interested in understanding the entire system: APIs, backend, architecture, testing, and everything that happens beyond the interface.

I like creating software that not only works, but is also clear, maintainable, and truly useful to the people who use it.

Outside of code, I enjoy chess, Medellín's rap scene, and continuing to learn about technology, engineering, and everything that combines **structure, creativity, and strategy**.
